# First Round — Coaching Card

Keep this page open beside the board for your first game of **Consulate — Control (Beginner)**. It is a learning aid, not a replacement for the mission guide, rulebook or current FAQ/errata.

## Before the round starts

- [ ] Confirm the mission, map, deployment, objective and pre-made Operator lists from the official mission material.
- [ ] Confirm your learning mode: companion app at Beginner speed, or the official No App variant.
- [ ] Put the Operator profiles, dice, line-of-sight tool and relevant tokens within reach.
- [ ] Agree that either player can pause to check a rule.

## Follow the round in order

Use the official sequence and the current rules for exactly who may activate and how many Operators may be activated in each phase:

1. **Attacker — first activation phase**
2. **Defender — first activation phase**
3. **Attacker — second activation phase**
4. **Defender — second activation phase**
5. **Upkeep**

A phase is not necessarily a single Operator activation. If several Operators activate during a phase, finish and record each activation separately. Use the session sheet to log the state after every activation.

## Ask these questions during each activation

### Before moving
- Which Operator is activating, and is that Operator still allowed to activate this round?
- What destination am I aiming for, and what route is legal?
- Have I counted movement points and any extra costs from the board?

### Before an attack
- Is the target legal and in line of sight according to the game's tool and rules?
- What range applies, and does the target have protection?
- Have I checked the relevant weapon, challenge and protection rules before rolling?

### Before using a gadget or other action
- Is this action available to this Operator in its current state?
- Does the profile or a special rule change the normal procedure?
- Have I completely resolved the effect before taking the next action?

If you cannot answer a question confidently, pause and check the official material. Do not guess a rule to keep the game moving.

## What to teach yourself in the first round

Keep the goal small: understand the board, movement, facing and line of sight. You do not need to optimise every decision or remember every gadget.

After each completed Operator activation, resolve the effects before moving on. For your first game, use the [First Game Session Sheet](./first-game-session-sheet.html) as a light phase summary. If you are deliberately collecting AI-test data, use the optional [Activation-by-Activation State Log](./activation-state-log.html) instead; it provides separate slots for multiple Operators in the same phase.

## At Upkeep

- [ ] Check the mission's victory conditions.
- [ ] Resolve the official end-of-round procedure and any required marker/token resets.
- [ ] If the game continues, confirm which Operators are available for the next round.
- [ ] Write down one rule you needed to look up.

**Rule priority:** if this coaching card differs from the official rulebook, mission material, Operator profile or current FAQ/errata, follow the official material.
